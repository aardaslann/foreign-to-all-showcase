# Foreign to All

*A pixel-art narrative adventure about language, culture, and belonging.*

You play as an alien who lands on Earth without a shared language. A gesture, a familiar-looking word, or the way someone says a sentence can give you enough of a clue to respond. Your field notebook keeps track of the words and phrases you discover.

Created by Arda Aslan in Godot.

## How it plays

In Berlin, finding bottles left beside bins is your first clue to how the local bottle-deposit system works. In London, the words are readable from the start, but you still have to work out what people mean from their tone and the situation.

The desktop beta begins in Berlin. From there, you can explore Istanbul, London, Paris, Moscow, Mumbai, Tokyo, and Mexico City in any order.
